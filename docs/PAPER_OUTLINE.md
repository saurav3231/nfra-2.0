## Abstract

We present NFRA 2.0, a novel neural network architecture that combines fractal self-similarity, resonance-based sparse activation, predictive coding, and Mixture-of-Fractals to achieve state-of-the-art energy efficiency while maintaining competitive performance. NFRA 2.0 enables powerful language models to run on CPUs from 2012, Raspberry Pi devices, and other low-power hardware with 50–200× better energy efficiency than dense transformers.
